Trustee

Live: https://www.trustee.com.ng Role: Project Manager Sector: Financial inclusion, Nigeria's informal economy

Product

Digitises cooperative daily savings (Ajo) and builds a Financial Identity for small businesses.

Daily savings through a local agent or directly from a phone
Dedicated business account number
Cash flow tracking and simple bookkeeping
Trust Score based on savings discipline, business activity, and repayment behaviour
Agent programme with commissions
Mini loans planned, not yet available

Savings are held by a registered cooperative society. Trustee is not a bank.
