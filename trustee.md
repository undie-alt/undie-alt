Trustee

Financial identity for Nigeria's informal economy trustee.com.ng · Role: Project Manager

Overview

Trustee digitises cooperative daily savings (Ajo) and turns everyday business activity into a Financial Identity. Traders, tailors, mechanics, vendors, and salon owners build a record that can later support access to credit.

Product Scope
Daily savings through a local agent or directly from a phone
Business account number so customers can pay professionally
Cash flow tracking and bookkeeping for income and expenses
Trust Score based on savings discipline, business activity, and repayment behaviour
Agent programme where community agents help businesses join and earn commissions
Mini loans planned for a later phase
Compliance Position

Savings are held by a registered cooperative society. Trustee is not a bank. Loans are not yet available.

My Role
Project Manager, working with engineers from the Guru Innovation Hub cohort trainings who built and deployed the product
Results
Live product covering savings, business accounts, bookkeeping, Trust Score, and an agent signup flow
