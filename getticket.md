Product

Event ticketing and voting platform. Organizers create an event, set ticket tiers (free, paid, VIP), and share one link per event. Attendees pay by bank transfer or cash and receive a QR code ticket. Gate staff scan tickets from any phone browser.

Organizer features: real-time sales by tier, team access with scan-only permissions, payout tracking, paid and free voting campaigns.
