getticket.ng

Event ticketing and voting for Nigerian organizers getticket.ng · Role: Project Manager · Owner: Guru Innovation Hub

Overview

getticket.ng lets organizers sell tickets and run paid or free votes from a shareable event page. Attendees pay by bank transfer or cash and enter with a QR code.

Product Scope
Event setup: name, date, venue, and ticket tiers (free, paid, VIP)
One link per event: a branded page to share on WhatsApp and social media
QR check-in: the scanner runs in any phone browser, including low-cost Android phones
Organizer dashboard: real-time sales by tier and payout tracking
Team access: gate staff get scan-only permissions and cannot see revenue
Voting campaigns: paid or free
My Role
Project Manager, working with engineers from the Guru Innovation Hub cohort trainings who built and deployed the product
Results
Live product with ticketing, voting, QR check-in, and organizer payouts
Context

Delivered within Guru Innovation Hub, where I managed 30+ team members across hubs and a functional budget of about ₦50 million.
