Moorral

Business school and management platform for Nigerian women entrepreneurs moorral.com · Role: Project Manager · Owner: Guru Innovation Hub

Overview

Moorral gives women in informal trades practical business education and the tools to run their businesses from one phone app. It targets tailors, bakers, hair stylists, caterers, makeup artists, and traders in Tier-2 Nigerian cities. The platform is free to start.

Product Scope
Six learning tracks: Fashion and Tailoring, Hair Styling, Makeup and Beauty, Baking and Pastries, Food and Cooking, Trading and Retail
Business suite: bookkeeping, product catalog, online store, customer records, bookings, measurement book, message templates
Learning path: each user picks a trade and follows a path of short video lessons, then applies it with the built-in tools
My Role
Project Manager for the product, working with engineers from the Guru Innovation Hub cohort trainings who built and deployed it
Oversaw programme strategy and due diligence
Led the CcHUB EdTech Fellowship application
Results
